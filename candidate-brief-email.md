## The ask

A public library wants a staff-facing dashboard to help the front desk manage day-to-day operations. Here's what they've asked for:

- **See how members are using the library.** Staff should be able to view the full list of members along with engagement stats — how many books each member has returned, their highest and average ratings, and whether they've ever returned something late.
- **Add new members at the front desk.** When someone signs up, staff should be able to add them right there.
- **Check members out books using the library's barcode scanners.** The desk workflow is: scan the member's library card, then scan one or more book barcodes, then complete the checkout.
- **Prevent checkout for members with an overdue book.** If a member already has something overdue, staff shouldn't be able to check out anything new to them until it's resolved.

They've also mentioned that down the line they'd like to open this API up to a partner reading app — worth keeping in mind, even if it's not the focus today.

## What you're building

- A backend API, in any language/framework you're comfortable with.
- A React frontend that staff will use day-to-day.

There's no existing database or schema for this — you'll design the data model as part of your solution.

## Format & timing

This is a live session, not a take-home — plan for about 2 hours:

- **20 minutes — planning.** You'll ask us anything about how the library actually operates, and talk through your approach before writing any code — including how you'd prioritize across everything above.
- **90 minutes — build.** You'll implement, at whatever pace and in whatever order makes sense to you. You will not finish everything in 90 minutes, and that's expected — we're as interested in what you choose to prioritize (and what you deliberately set aside) as in the code itself.
- **10 minutes — wrap-up.** You'll walk us through what you built, what you'd do next, and we'll dig into a couple of your decisions together.
